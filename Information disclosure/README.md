## Information Disclosure

Methodology notes, not individual writeups, for this category — explained below.

**Why these aren't writeups:** a writeup is meant to demonstrate reasoning —
here's the hypothesis I formed, the chain of steps I took to test it, and why
the impact is real. Most of this category's apprentice labs are single-technique
recognition drills: break a type, read the error, done. There isn't a multi-step
investigative chain to show off, and five near-identical "broke a parameter,
found a stack trace" writeups back to back would read as repetitive filler
rather than five distinct demonstrations of skill. That volume is better spent
as one consolidated methodology reference than five thin portfolio entries.

**Why they live here and not in Bug-bounty-writeups:** this repo is for
distilled, reusable technique — cheat sheets and checklists meant to be
consulted mid-hunt, not narrated proof of a specific solve. That's exactly what
this category produced: a checklist of "where to look and what counts as
sensitive," applicable across any target, rather than a story about one
specific lab's specific bug.
